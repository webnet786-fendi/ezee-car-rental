      Questions

      Before you ask

      Can I take the self-drive car outside JB, or to Singapore?

      Yes. The minimum rental depends on how far you go: 1 day within JB, 2 days for Singapore, Melaka, Seremban or KL, 3 days for Pahang, 4 days for Perak, Ipoh, Cameron Highlands, the East Coast or the northern states, and 5 days for Perlis or Langkawi. Pick your destination in the price box and the days adjust automatically. During the year-end school holidays and on public holidays the minimum is 3 days for every destination.

      Can you pick up from any address in Singapore?

      Yes — homes, offices, hotels and Changi Airport, island-wide. The same price applies to most SG addresses; we confirm your exact quote on WhatsApp.

      How much luggage fits?

      Comfortably four passengers with four large suitcases, or six passengers with cabin bags. Golf bags and strollers are no problem — just mention them when booking.

      Do you do late-night or early-morning rides?

      Yes. Pickups between 11 pm and 7 am carry a small S$30 surcharge, and red-eye flight pickups are flight-tracked so we’re there even when you land early.

      Can you get me to Senai Airport for a cheap AirAsia flight?

      Yes, and it is one of our most popular runs. Pickup from your Singapore address, drop at Senai departures, S$150 all-in. We track your flight, so for an early departure we advise the pickup time and for the return we are waiting at arrivals even if you land late.

      How do I pay from Singapore?

      PayNow for the deposit and balance — no bank transfer fees, no currency hassle. DuitNow, Malaysian bank transfer and cash work too.

      Is the crossing time included in a charter?

      Charter hours run from pickup to drop-off. We schedule around peak causeway hours where possible and advise the best departure time when you book.

      Will it really be the same car?

      Yes. Every car on this page is one we own. You book a specific car and that car is what you get. There is no fleet substitution and no “or similar”. If it is ever unavailable we tell you before you pay a deposit.

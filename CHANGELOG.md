- **Three-tier self-drive pricing (17 Oct 2026)**: every car now has exactly three rates, 1 day, 7 days (80% of the day rate per day) and 30 days (50%), shown as a per-day price with a small "RM total for n days · Chat for best price!" line under the 7 and 30 day rows in the price box, the vehicle overlay and the rate card. Axia 2nd Gen 125 and Bezza 1.0 (New) 140 to match the owner's Axia and Bezza; Vellfire 549 / 439 / 275 (the 3–6 day step is gone), Bezza 140 / 112 / 70. Hourly rates removed from every car and from admin.
- **Holiday minimum**: `settings.holidays` (admin → Bookings → Public holidays) and `settings.peakMinDays` (3). A self-drive rental that starts on, or runs into, a public holiday or a peak-season day within the first 3 days must be at least 3 days; the days picker enforces it and the note, overlay, FAQ, minimum-days popup and rate card say so. Seeded with Deepavali 8 Nov 2026, Christmas, New Year 2027 and Chinese New Year 6–7 Feb 2027.
- **Monthly rule (17 Oct 2026)**: from 30 days every car costs 40% of its 1-day rate per day (a `30+` tier on all 46 cars; the `monthly` service price on Vellfire and Bezza is set to 30 × that). The falling-tier cap now runs on every car, not only the surveyed ones (Honda BR-V 7-day rate fixed). Four 22–29 day fixed totals (S70, Civic FC, HR-V, Accord) were lifted to the monthly per-day × 29 so 29 days is never cheaper than 30.
- **Prices set to the Johor Bahru average (17 Oct 2026)**: the 25 models in the JB rate survey now start at the midpoint of the lowest and highest published JB rate, with the owner's own figures for Persona 170, Alza (new) 250, Civic FE 450, Alphard 549 and Staria 550; Bezza 140 / 125 (2–6 d) / 110 (7+ d); Vellfire unchanged at 549 / 499 / 450. Every longer tier is capped so a rental never costs more per day than a shorter one (the imported 4–6 and 7–9 day tiers used to rise above the 1-day rate). Headline, page title, description and schema price range now say "from RM110 a day" (lowest 1-day car is the Axia 2nd Gen).
- **Fleet rail auto-scrolls** right to left at about 30 px a second and loops without a jump (the cards are rendered twice). It pauses while the visitor touches, hovers, scrolls or focuses the rail or opens a car, resumes 4 s after they let go, and stays off when the system asks for reduced motion.

- Custom domain **https://ezee.my** (GitHub Pages CNAME, HTTPS enforced; github.io and www redirect). Share image and the ledger link in alert emails point at the domain.
- Booking script v5: email alert to the owner for every website request (Ref, trip, price, ledger link); duplicate taps within 2 minutes reuse the same Ref; more than 40 website requests in 10 minutes are dropped; honeypot field on the price box.

- Agreement form accepts prefill parameters (ref, name, phone, car, start, time, days, rate, dest, loc, deposit) and starts a fresh form from them.
- Calendar hold: the price box reads confirmed bookings from the ledger (`?action=busy`) and shows a car as **On request** when the requested dates overlap a confirmed booking of that car (self-drive requests are blocked by any confirmed booking; chauffeured requests only by multi-day ones). The WhatsApp button stays active ("Ask about these dates on WhatsApp") and the message carries a note.
- Booking control: every WhatsApp tap in the price box is logged as **Waiting Confirmation** with a reference (`Ref: EZ-yymmdd-XXX`, also placed in the WhatsApp message). Backend = Google Apps Script + Google Sheet under the owner's personal Gmail (`gas/Code.gs`, setup in `gas/README.md`). New private `ledger.html` (status Waiting → Confirmed → Completed / Cancelled / No-show, customer, phone, deposit, notes, history, manual bookings, tiles for waiting / upcoming / this month). Admin gets a Bookings tab for the web-app URL (`settings.bookingsUrl`; empty = logging off). Public `?action=busy` endpoint prepared for the calendar hold.
- Language switcher (EN default / BM / 中文) in the header of the landing page and the rate card. Choice is remembered in the browser (`ezee-lang`) and can be forced with `?lang=ms` / `?lang=zh`. Translations live in `i18n.js`: static text by `data-i18n` key, fleet data (services, notes, inclusions, zones) by exact English string with English fallback, so new cars or services added in admin appear in English until a translation is added. WhatsApp messages stay in English for the owner.
